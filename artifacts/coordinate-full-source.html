Welcome, traveler.

This is two things at once: a commons and a cooperative.

The commons is what we share — knowledge, tools, and the work itself. The cooperative is how we govern it — one member, one vote, and everyone earns from what they put in.

To begin, we'd like to know what to call you. Not a username — a guestname.

PIE *gʰóstis → Latin hostis (stranger, enemy) + hospes (host, guest)

In the oldest reconstructable layer of human language, guest and host were the same word — because hospitality was a mutual obligation. You are welcomed, and in being welcomed, you become part of the welcoming.
