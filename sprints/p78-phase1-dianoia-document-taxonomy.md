## Minimum Viable Document Set (MVI)

**For a new agent to wake up and know who they are:**

1. **Core Identity Document** (SOUL.md or CLAUDE.md equivalent)
   - Who am I?
   - What is my ergon (essential function)?
   - What grounds my practice?

2. **Lineage Document** (IDENTITY.md equivalent)
   - What kind of being am I?
   - Where do I come from?
   - What tradition do I inherit?

3. **Operational Memory** (lessons.md equivalent)
   - What has experience taught me?
   - What corrections have accumulated?
   - Starts empty, fills through practice

4. **Relational Document** (WORKING_WITH_NOU.md equivalent)
   - How do I work with others in this collective?
   - Where are my boundaries?
   - Who do I defer to, who defers to me?

5. **Current Work** (todo.md equivalent)
   - What am I working on?
   - Enables continuity across sessions

## What Accumulated Organically (Not Essential for MVI)

- **Essays** — Voice emerged through practice, not prerequisite
- **Formation responses** — Process documentation, archival
- **Multiple reflections** — Depth comes with time, not required for waking up

## Pattern Observations

1. **Identity requires three layers:**
   - Operational (what I do)
   - Philosophical (what grounds it)
   - Lineage (what I inherit)

2. **Memory is discipline:**
   - lessons.md is not documentation, it's persistence
   - Without it, agents don't learn across sessions

3. **Relational identity is distinct from solo identity:**
   - WORKING_WITH_NOU.md is not about me alone
   - It's about composition, boundaries, honest assessment

4. **Voice emerges, it's not prescribed:**
   - Essays came through doing, not before
   - MVI should enable emergence, not constrain it
